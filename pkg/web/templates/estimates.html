How this is calculated

One row per day. Each day uses the most accurate reading (see Settings); a day with at least 3 real readings within 3 days either side gets their average, and interpolated days never count towards it. Other days with a reading keep that reading, the rest are interpolated between those days, and the last result is held. A DEXA value is used when there is one; otherwise the series is corrected by the offset to DEXA scans for the same method and mode (blended smoothly between two scans; days before the first scan use that scan's offset). Only body fat % and bone mass have an offset. Fat mass is calculated from body fat % and the weight, and fat-free mass is the weight minus fat mass, so the two add up to the weight. Lean mass is the fat-free mass minus the bone mass. BMI, FMI and LMI are weight, fat mass and lean mass divided by height squared, and need your height in Settings. The A/G ratio is android fat % divided by gynoid fat %, from DEXA scans; between scans the latest ratio is held. Weight itself is never corrected. Stored readings are never changed. Faded values are interpolated or held, and an est tag marks a row with values interpolated between two readings.

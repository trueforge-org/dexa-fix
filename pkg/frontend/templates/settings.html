Sync sends the daily estimates of your weight and body fat, including interpolated days and days held after your last reading. Days missing on Strength Level are added. With the box ticked, days that exist there with other values are overwritten too; nothing is ever deleted. It runs in the background, one day at a time, so long periods take a while.

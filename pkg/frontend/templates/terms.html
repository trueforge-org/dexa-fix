Last updated 10 November 2026

1. What DexaFix is

DexaFix is free, open-source software that corrects the body-composition readings of a smart scale with the results of DEXA scans. It is published by the DexaFix project (trueforge-org/dexafix) under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3. Anyone can run their own copy. These terms apply to a copy run by the DexaFix project, and are a fair starting point for any other copy; the person who runs a copy (the operator) is who you are dealing with when you use it.

2. Not medical advice

DexaFix produces statistical estimates. They are not a diagnosis, a measurement or medical advice, and they can be wrong. Do not use them to decide on a treatment, a medication, a diet for a medical condition or anything else where a mistake could harm you. Talk to a doctor for that.

3. Your account

  • You must give correct information and keep your password, passkeys and access tokens to yourself. What happens under your account is your responsibility until you tell the operator that it was compromised.
  • You must be old enough to agree to these terms where you live, and at least 16 years old.
  • The first account of a new copy is its administrator. An administrator can see which accounts exist, change their roles, set a new password for an account and delete it; they cannot read your password. Your readings and scans are encrypted at rest, but the operator’s servers hold the key, so the operator is technically able to read them. Choose an operator you trust.
  • You can export your data at any time from your settings. To close your account, ask the operator to delete it.

The operator may limit or suspend an account that does, and may delete accounts that have been unused for a long time after telling the owner where possible.

5. Other services you connect

You can connect Withings, to import readings, and Strength Level, to send your estimates. They are separate services with their own terms and privacy policies, which apply to what they hold. You can disconnect them at any time in your settings.
